How we work.

Pump Paper is a practice tool. Here is what it may do, what it never does and how you can check that yourself. As of version 1.5.0.

What never happensPermissionsVerify the downloadCoinContact

What never happens

  • No wallet. Pump Paper connects to no wallet, never asks for a seed or key and cannot send a transaction.
  • No buying on its own. Every buy is your click. Only your rules for an open position run automatically: target, stop, time, all with play money.
  • No server of ours, no account with us. Your data lives in your browser. Details are in the privacy page.
  • Read-only to the website. The account page may read your paper account from the extension but cannot change anything.
  • No promises. Paper profits are not real profits. The site names no returns, and the lab always shows results with costs and warnings.

Verify the download

The download always comes from the releases on GitHub, never from a chat or a direct message.

SHA-256 of zip 1.5.0: d509e3655fa44a4c12b391551456a5fdd1a928c9b06f3d76b8b31dc5346e3ba5. Check on Mac or Linux with shasum -a 256 Pump-Paper.zip, on Windows with certutil -hashfile Pump-Paper.zip SHA256.

The code in the zip is obfuscated so it can't simply be copied. What it may do is still set by Chrome: only the permissions above.

If there is a coin

Pump Paper needs no coin and stays free. Should there be one, the only real address is on this website. Our bot never buys it, we promise no value and no returns, and we clear the legal situation before any real money.
