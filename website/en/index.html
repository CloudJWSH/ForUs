    How it works

    A paper profit should have been a real profit.

    Most paper traders use the price on the screen. Pump Paper uses the price your trade would really have gotten.

    Price

    Straight from the blockchain. Pump Paper reads the reserves of the bonding curve or the PumpSwap pool and calculates your amount from them. After migration it switches to the pool on its own.

    Fee

    Per coin, by market cap. Protocol, creator and pool fees come from pump.fun's fee config, not from a fixed number.

    1 s

    Delayed execution. Your trade fills after one second, at the price that holds then. Click late into a pump and you feel it on paper too.

    10 %

    Slippage limit like pump.fun. If the price runs further away in that time, the trade fails and still costs the priority fee. Target, stop and time sells have no limit and land below the mark in a crash.

    0.00204

    Account rent on the first buy. Just like a real token account, the first buy of a coin costs rent in SOL once.

    Who it's for

    For anyone who wants to practice before it costs.

    New to pump.fun

    See what the bonding curve, fees and slippage do to your money before you put real SOL in.

    With your own strategy

    Test fixed rules for target, stop and time over many trades. The history shows which exit reason makes you money and which costs it.

    With signals or a bot

    Check calls from groups or your own bot on paper, with the same costs as real, instead of aping in right away.

    Compared

    Others estimate. Pump Paper does the math.

    Many paper trading tools take the price on screen and subtract a flat fee. On pump.fun the gap between estimated and real is often several percent per trade.

    To be honest: what paper doesn't capture

    • Other buyers landing in the same block ahead of you, and bots that exploit your buy.
    • Congested networks where real transactions only land after several tries.
    • What it feels like to watch real money drop. Losses are easier to sit through on paper.

    Install

    In Chrome in two minutes.

    Pump Paper is not in the Chrome Web Store yet. Until then you load it as an unpacked extension.

    1. Download and unzip

      Download the zip on the right and unzip it. Put the folder somewhere permanent, for example in Documents. Chrome loads the extension from that folder.Mac: double-click the zip in Downloads. Windows: right-click the zip, then “Extract All”.

    2. Open extensions

      Type chrome://extensions into the address bar and turn on Developer mode at the top right.

    3. Load unpacked

      Click “Load unpacked” and pick the Pump Paper folder. Pin it from the puzzle icon so your account is one click away.

    4. Open pump.fun

      Open any coin. The panel with the yellow PAPER tag appears below the trade box. Done.

    FAQ

    Short answers.

    Do I need a wallet?

    No. Pump Paper connects to no wallet and cannot send real transactions. It only reads prices from the blockchain.

    What does it cost?

    Nothing. You trade with play money and set your starting capital in the options (default 10 SOL and 1,000 USDC).

    How close is it to real trades?

    Price, fee, delay, slippage, priority fee and account rent are included. Other buyers landing in the same block ahead of you are not modeled. On very hot coins real fills are often a little worse still.

    Why is my paper profit smaller than the chart shows?

    Because Pump Paper prices buy and sell on the curve. 1.25 % fee each way, the price impact of your own amount, priority fees and account rent all come off. A coin has to rise a few percent before you are in profit.

    Can I start over?

    Yes. In the options, “Konto zurücksetzen” (reset account) sets balance, positions and history back to your starting capital. Save your history as CSV first if you want to keep it.

    Does it work in Brave, Edge or Arc?

    Yes, in every Chromium-based browser via “Load unpacked”. Firefox and Safari are not supported.

    Where is my data?

    Only in your browser. There is no account and no server. You can save your history as CSV at any time.

    Does it work after migration?

    Yes. As soon as a coin leaves the curve, Pump Paper keeps going with the PumpSwap pool, including open positions.

    Is this financial advice?

    No. Pump Paper is a practice tool. Memecoins are extremely risky, and a paper profit is no guarantee of real profits.

    Does it run on GMGN, Axiom or Photon?

    Soon. The version for GMGN, DexScreener, Axiom, Photon, BullX and Padre is built and comes with the next download. Until then Pump Paper runs on pump.fun.

    Is there a Pump Paper coin?

    No, not yet. If there ever is one, the only real mint address will be listed here on this website. Any other coin with the name is not ours.
