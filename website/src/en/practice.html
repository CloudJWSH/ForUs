Practice

Paper only pays off if you take it seriously.

Pump Paper prices trades as close to real as it can. A few habits help you actually learn from it. This is not financial advice, just a suggestion for how to practice.

Habits

  1. Practice with the amounts you would really use

    Set starting capital and buy buttons in the options to match your real account. With 10 SOL of play money and 1 SOL buys you learn nothing about an account with 1 SOL.

  2. Use your real settings

    Set priority fee and slippage the way you use them on pump.fun. Only then do the same buys fail on paper as for real.

  3. Set rules before you buy

    Set target, stop and time limit for new positions in the options before you open the first coin. Changing rules mid-trade trains the wrong habit.

  4. Keep the coin page open

    With the page open, Pump Paper checks target and stop on every live price, in the background only every 30 seconds. The stop calculator shows what that means in a crash.

  5. Take failed trades seriously

    A buy that fails on slippage is not bad luck on paper, it tells you that you were late. The history lists it with its reason.

  6. Review once a week

    Save the history as CSV and look at it in a spreadsheet: result per reason (target, stop, time, manual), total fees and gas, average holding time. The guide explains every column.

  7. Play in rounds

    After a week, keep the CSV and reset the account. That way you can compare rounds and see whether you get better.

  8. Write it down first, then test

    Write your strategy down in the strategy lab before you practice it on pump.fun. Test it there with several seeds, then compare with your CSV whether you stuck to the rules.

Week plan

Seven exercises, one a day. You can write a note for each, it stays only in this browser.

  1. Monday: just watch

    Open ten fresh coins and buy nothing. For each, write down at which market cap you would have bought and why. In the evening, check what became of them.

  2. Tuesday: set the stop first

    Before every buy, write the stop in percent and set it in the panel, then don't change it. In the evening, count how often you stuck to it.

  3. Wednesday: halve the size

    Every buy at half your usual size. Then compare slippage and failed buys with the day before, and how calm you stayed on losses.

  4. Thursday: buy only by the rules

    Take a strategy from the lab and only buy coins that match it. Skip and note every buy that doesn't.

  5. Friday: five trades at most

    No more buys than that today. It forces you to take only the coins that really match your rules.

  6. Saturday: review

    Save the history as CSV and load it on the account page. Read the tips, look at the result by reason and hold time.

  7. Sunday: sharpen the rules

    Bring your trades into the lab with “Use as strategy”, change one thing and retest with two other seeds. Keep only what holds in both.

Limits of paper. Pump Paper models other buyers landing in the same block ahead of you only through the delay. On very hot coins real fills are often a little worse still. A paper profit is no guarantee of real profits.
