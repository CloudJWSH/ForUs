Strategy lab

Your rules, tested on paper.

Write down your own strategy and run it against simulated pump.fun coins or against your own paper trades. The math is the same as in Pump Paper: real bonding curve, 1.25 % fee, priority fee, delay and slippage.

Paper only. The lab buys nothing and gives no signals. Pump Paper never trades on its own: you decide every buy. A good result here is a hint, not a promise for real money.

Save and share as a file

Your strategy is a small JSON file in the open pump-paper-strategy format. Pump Paper is meant to read these files later, so the extension can show you when your rules match. You still do every buy yourself.

Apply to your own trades

Load the CSV from the Pump Paper history. The lab shows which of your buys the entry rule would have allowed by market cap, and how those trades did. Curve progress and age are not in the CSV, so they don't count here. The file stays in this tab.

The format

Every number has limits, unknown fields are ignored. Wallets, keys or server addresses have no place in the format. Examples live in the repo under website/strategien, the schema in strategy.schema.json.

Format version 1, as of {{date}}. New fields only come with a new version number.

How the simulation works. Every simulated coin starts on a fresh bonding curve. Dev and snipers buy in the first block and sell again soon, often the dev sells too. After that, quiet phases, hype and sell-offs take turns. It is a rough model, not a forecast: real coins can behave very differently. The same seed gives the same coins, so you can compare two strategies fairly.
